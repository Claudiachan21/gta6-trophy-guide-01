An offline guide and tracker for all 55 Grand Theft Auto VI trophies on PlayStation 5: official names, descriptions and grades, a cited guide for every trophy, a roadmap to the platinum, a saved checklist with filters, export/import and a spoiler-free mode. No network needed — it runs entirely from its own files.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — or serve `app/` with any static file server and open `app/index.html`.

## What's inside

- **All 55 PS5 trophies** with official names, descriptions and grades (1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze), each with a guide written in our own words and cited to its source.
- **Roadmap to the platinum** in 4 stages: story, character questlines, activities & collectibles, then heists, money & moments.
- **Tracker** — mark trophies earned; progress is saved in your browser, and you can export it to a file and import it back.
- **Filters** — story, missable, collectibles, activities, online, earned and to-do, plus search and sorting.
- **Spoiler mode** — hides the names and descriptions of secret story trophies until you earn them.
- **Missable flags** — only one trophy in the whole list can be missed (Positive Mental Attitude, Chapter 1); everything else is still earnable in free-roam after the story.
